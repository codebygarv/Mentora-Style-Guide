# Mentora App Context & Specifications (Brain)

## 1. App Overview
- **Name**: Mentora
- **Tagline**: Connect. Learn. Grow.
- **Core Concept**: Mentora is a mobile mentorship platform connecting students and learners with experienced industry professionals for short, high-impact, 1:1 mentorship sessions.
- **Design Language**: Electric Violet & Royal Indigo token system with clean light porcelain and deep slate glassmorphism. Uses `Inter` for clean technical and UI elements, with `Plus Jakarta Sans` and `Playfair Display` for impactful editorial headings.

## 2. Core Features
- **Authentication**: Onboarding carousel, register/login with role selection (Mentee / Mentor), OTP verification, password reset.
- **Discovery & Search**: Browse verified mentors by skills (React, Node, AI/ML, System Design), rating, hourly rates, and company.
- **Smart Matching**: AI matching score based on career goals (e.g. "Full-Stack Developer", "96% Match").
- **Scheduling & Booking**: Calendar slot picker, session types (15m Quick Advice, 30m Standard, 45m Mock Interview, 60m Deep Dive).
- **Payment & Checkout**: Seamless Razorpay/UPI/Card checkout flow with upfront fee breakdown.
- **Live 1-on-1 Video Session**: Integrated video call interface with mentor stream, participant PIP, countdown timer, and control bar.
- **Session Notes & Rating**: Action items checklist, review and rating submission.
- **Mentor Dashboard**: Mentor analytics, today's schedule, gross & net earnings tracker, and availability management.

## 3. Visual Reference Guidelines
- **Colors**: Built around Royal Indigo (`#6366F1`), Violet (`#4F46E5`), and Deep Midnight Slate (`#0F172A`), accented by Gold Amber (`#F59E0B`) for ratings and Emerald (`#10B981`) for online status.
- **Cards & Layouts**: Glassmorphic frosted panels (`backdrop-filter: blur(...)`), gentle 24px-36px corner radiuses, floating pill buttons, and subtle multi-layered drop shadows.
- **Interactions**: Micro-animations, active tab indicators, hover lifts, pulse indicators for live mentorship, and custom mobile scrollbars.
